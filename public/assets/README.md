# SMART WASH — Instructional Media Directory

This directory hosts demonstration video loops for each WHO handwashing step.

### Expected Video Files:
Place short (5–10s) looped MP4 clips corresponding to each WHO handwashing step into this folder:
- `step_1.mp4` — Step 1: Rub palms together (Palm to Palm)
- `step_2.mp4` — Step 2: Right palm over left dorsum with interlaced fingers and vice versa
- `step_3.mp4` — Step 3: Palm to palm with fingers interlaced
- `step_4.mp4` — Step 4: Backs of fingers to opposing palms with fingers interlocked
- `step_5.mp4` — Step 5: Rotational rubbing of left thumb clasped in right palm and vice versa
- `step_6.mp4` — Step 6: Rotational rubbing, backwards and forwards with clasped fingers in palm

### Graceful Fallback Behavior:
If video files are absent, the SMART WASH Kiosk automatically displays an instructional icon card with WHO step titles and recommended durations without throwing errors or breaking the interface layout.
